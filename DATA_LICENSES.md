# Atlas data and redistribution terms

The MIT license covers original project code, not the data in `data/` or derived anatomy in the offline HTML.

## Allen-derived assets

`atlas-data.js`, `atlas-meshes.js`, `allen_structure_tree.json`, `mesh-volume-100um.*`, `native-region-volumes.json`, `research-reference.js`, and `merfish-region-map.json` contain or derive from Allen-CCF-2020 / 20230630 annotation and ontology. Native source: 10-µm annotation; browsing/reference/mesh display is coarser as documented. The geometric fingerprint is retained; removing private network values does not change anatomy.

Source portal: https://portal.brain-map.org/ . Official ABC download location: https://allen-brain-cell-atlas.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/ .

Allen Institute Terms of Use: https://alleninstitute.org/legal/terms-of-use . Citation Policy: https://alleninstitute.org/legal/citation-policy/ . The general terms permit research/noncommercial use and distribution subject to their conditions and citation requirements; commercial redistribution requires permission unless a specific asset license states otherwise. They also contain rights-to-improvements conditions. Read the complete applicable terms and any asset-specific notice before reuse. These assets have **not** been relicensed under MIT.

Reference: Wang Q et al. The Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework: A 3D Reference Atlas. Cell. 2020;181:936–953.e20. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.04.007 . Consult Allen's current citation policy for the particular data release you use.

User MRI templates, statistical images, H5AD matrices, connectivity volumes and research outputs are not distributed. Optional local bridges require the user to acquire their own authorized source files.

## Public-demo integrity

Generated examples are explicitly synthetic. Private network presets and all user result pages were excluded. No original simulated or measured research-study outputs were copied into this source release.

Swanson geometry and result pages are not bundled in this first beta. Future integration must retain IBL/flatmap source attribution and verify their asset-specific terms independently.
